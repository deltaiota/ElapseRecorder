# Elapse Recorder 1.13.5

This release removes two sources of foreground contention while recording latency-sensitive applications.

## Performance

- Reuse persistent ScreenCaptureKit streams instead of rebuilding a screenshot session on every capture interval.
- Pause and cancel HEVC segment compaction while a monitored application is frontmost, then resume safely after switching away.
- Hold startup compaction until the whitelist and frontmost application are known, preventing heavy recovery work immediately after a computer restart.

## Reliability

- Requeue interrupted segment jobs without losing their durable HEIC source frames.
- Preserve exact frame-count, timestamp, dimension, and boundary-frame verification for completed HEVC segments.
