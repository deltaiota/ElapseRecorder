# Elapse Recorder 1.13.7

Fixed sustained high CPU usage during storage recovery and background HEVC compaction. Storage checks no longer run on top of one another or repeatedly scan the recording history. Compaction now adapts to CPU usage without changing capture intervals, resolution, or quality.

On an M1 Pro, recording a 2560 × 1504 window at a one-second interval averaged 11.6% CPU. Startup and recovery of a 477,917-frame library averaged 8.2% over 90 seconds. Short peaks can still exceed 20%.
